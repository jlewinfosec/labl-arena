# LABL Arena · Board Member quick guide (v1.0.0)

One tablet, one PIN. This page is also in the app: Board Member panel › Settings › Help.

## Before you leave home
- Tablet and speaker charged. Open **LABL** from the home-screen icon.
- Settings › Backup: *Storage* says **Protected**. Settings › Voice callouts › **Test sound** through the speaker.
- Roster complete (age chips tapped). Day plan built: **Build a full day**, or **Build a story day** for a special event.
- Open the **Packing list** on the Day plan and tick as you load the car.

## Opening the day
1. Arena › **Teams**: tap everyone who's here, set blasters, **Draw teams**.
2. **Run**: read the opening talk, tap **Briefing done**.
3. The first block's rules card opens: read it out, tap **Rules read. Start the countdown**.

## Running a mode
- The clock runs itself: one-minute warning, last ten, horn — tones and the voice.
- After the horn: tap the winner (stat modes: tap tags and outs, then the confirm button), pick how it ended.
- **Next Up** shows the players the next round. Tap it once to get your controls back. **Start round N** is ready at once.
- A block ends at the second win; the reload break follows. **Next** brings the next block and its team draw.

## Objective stations (same tablet)
- **Mini-game station**: pick the game, Start; players tap **RED plays / BLUE plays**. A win ends the round; a fail locks that side for 30 s.
- **Run Hardpoint here**: tap **RED HOLDS / BLUE HOLDS / CONTESTED** as the live hill changes hands.
- **Score Territories here**: at the horn, tap each marker's owner.
- The result comes back to the run screen: tap to log it.

## Walk-ins
**+ Walk-in** on the run screen: name, Add. They join the pool and the side that keeps the balance.

## Fixing a round
Rounds tab › **Edit** on the round: winner, how it ended, who played, tags and outs, a disputed note. Standings recompute.

## Ending the day
- The last item is **Dart sweep & teardown**: darts in the buckets, barriers down, props counted against the packing list.
- On **That's the day**: tap **Send backup & results to Google Drive**, pick Drive in the share sheet. The **Day Champion** is on the same card.

## If something goes wrong
- The app won't open: Chrome → https://jlewinfosec.github.io/labl-arena/ → install again → Settings › Restore a backup.
- The screen locked: the clock keeps running; unlock with the PIN from the run screen.
- The voice went quiet: Settings › Voice callouts › Test sound; check the speaker is paired; voice can be set to Tone per callout.
