# Mini-game fail wording · EP → LABL (v0.6.0)

Every fail now shows a cause tag, a title and one line. The cause tags: ⏱ CLOCK RAN OUT · ✖ TOO MANY SLIPS · ■ WALKED AWAY · ⏻ SCREEN LOST · ✖ NO GOOD. Every fail means the same thing: the station locks on this tablet for 30 s (Standard) or 60 s (Hard).

| Game | EP fail title · EP story (Standard) | LABL title · LABL line |
|---|---|---|
| Prime the air line | Line burst · A seam split and a jet of hot sludge caught you. (Take 1 Hit) | Line still dead · The air line never primed. Clear the station: it's locked for a moment. |
| Crack the password | Terminal locked · The trace caught you mid-hack and locked onto your radios. (No radio 10 min) | Password held · Too many wrong guesses and the screen locked itself. Step off and let it cool down. |
| Pick the lock | Pick snapped · The tension wrench slipped and bent your picks out of true. (Pay 10 Building Materials) | Pick snapped · The last pick broke off in the lock. The station's locked for a moment; your team can try again after. |
| Bypass the circuit | Circuit fried · The panel bit back. The jolt ran up your arm. (Take 1 Hit) | Circuit tripped · The breaker popped and the panel went dark. Give it a moment to reset. |
| Patch the vest | Patient lost · The stitches tore and the bleeding got worse. (Patient takes 1 Hit) | Vest still torn · The patch didn't hold. The repair station locks for a moment. |
| Punch the gate code | Seal jammed · The decon system read you as a breach and vented spores… (Infected) | Gate code rejected · Wrong code one time too many. The keypad is locked for a moment. |
| Rewire the panel | Panel shorted · The panel blew a fuse and the arc caught your hand. (1 Hit + 10 BM) | Panel shorted · A wire went to the wrong lamp and the panel tripped. It resets in a moment. |

| Hold the signal | Signal lost · The signal slipped away and the antenna feed arced into your hand. (1 Hit) | Signal lost · The signal slipped away before the upload filled. The radio locks for a moment. |

| Field surgery | Bled out · The artery slipped the clamp… (No healing 5 min) | Patient lost · Too slow on the table. The medic station locks for a moment. |
| Ghost the patrol | Spotted · A sentry clipped you as you ran for the wall. (1 Hit) | Spotted · The patrol saw you cross. The station locks for a moment. |
| Smash it open | Lock held · The hammer bounced off the hasp and into your shin. (1 Hit, shout CRASH) | Lock held · Every swing missed the sweet spot. The lock's still shut, and the station locks for a moment. |

The intro's "If you fail" fold now reads: "<title>. The station locks on this tablet for 0:30 (Standard). Nothing else. Tagged while playing? Tap I got tagged and step away: no lockout."
